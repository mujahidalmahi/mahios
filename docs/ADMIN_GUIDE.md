# MahiOS Admin Dashboard & CMS Guide

The **MahiOS Admin Dashboard** (`/admin`) is a dynamic content management system offering complete administrative freedom across all retro applications, biography chapters, and system settings.

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Access

1. **URL**: Navigate to `/admin` or press `Ctrl + Shift + A` from the desktop.
2. **Authentication Gate**: Direct access is protected. Log in via `/admin/login` using your administrator credentials registered in Supabase Authentication.
3. **Universal Command Palette (`Ctrl + K` / `Cmd + K`)**: Instant keyboard search and jump to any of the 25+ studios.
4. **Mobile Navigation**: Hamburger menu button on screens `<768px` reveals a touch-optimized slide-out navigation drawer with live search filtering.

---

## 🛠️ Administrative Studios & Hubs

### 1. 📂 **Core & Career**
- **Overview Hub (`/admin`)**: System telemetry counters across 26 models, PostgreSQL health, and **1-Click JSON Backup Exporter**.
- **About Me (`/admin/content/about`)**: Narrative bio story, interactive taglines builder, interests tags, and experience metrics.
- **Career Roles (`/admin/content/experience`)**: Work history, company logo uploader, employment type chip picker, and TipTap role narrative editor.
- **Projects Directory (`/admin/content/projects`)**: Projects catalog with dynamic category chips, GitHub / live demo links, and screenshot galleries.
- **Skills & Tech Radar (`/admin/content/skills`)**: Skill proficiencies (0–100%), module categories, and icons.
- **Education (`/admin/content/education`)**: Academic degrees, leadership roles, activities builder, and credentials.
- **Milestones Timeline (`/admin/content/biography`)**: Chronological life chapters and learnings.
- **Achievements & Certs (`/admin/content/achievements`)**: Award certificates, credential IDs, and confetti triggers.
- **Resume Viewer (`/admin/content/resume`)**: PDF URL, ATS summary markdown, and download configurations.

---

### 2. 🧠 **Mindset & Vision**
- **Philosophy & Principles (`/admin/content/philosophy`)**: Mental models and guiding rules with custom domain categories.
- **Strategic Aims & Roadmap (`/admin/content/aim`)**: Goals with progress sliders (0–100%), milestones checklist builder, and status indicators.
- **Ideology & Tech Ethics (`/admin/content/ideology`)**: Worldview pillars and essays.
- **Dreamscape Manifestos (`/admin/content/dream`)**: Long-term vision manifestos with custom horizons (Decade, Lifetime, Civilizational).
- **3 Wishes for Humanity (`/admin/content/wishes`)**: 3 existential wishes with impact scopes and motivations.
- **Personal Hall of Fame (`/admin/content/favourites`)**: Curated tools, gear, books, cuisine, with 1–10 star ratings.

---

### 3. 🌐 **Social, Media & Comms**
- **Status Updates & Feed (`/admin/content/feed`)**: Live micro-posts with media previews, hashtags, and likes counters.
- **Digital ID Hub (`/admin/content/socials`)**: Verified digital links with custom channel categories and hex color accent pickers.
- **Media & Entertainment (`/admin/content/entertainment`)**: Video games, cinema, anime, books with reviews and favorite quotes.
- **Photo Archive (`/admin/content/gallery`)**: Photo albums with lightbox metadata.
- **Articles & Notes (`/admin/content/blog`)**: Long-form blog posts with TipTap rich text, reading time estimator, and cover photos.
- **Inbound Messages (`/admin/messages`)**: Visitor contact inquiries sent via the desktop `Mail_Client.exe`.

---

### 4. ⚙️ **OS Engine & Settings**
- **Desktop Apps Manager (`/admin/apps`)**: Register, reorder, show/hide, resize, or assign any of the 22 component views.
- **Terminal MS-DOS (`/admin/terminal`)**: Custom command prompt commands and responses.
- **BIOS Boot Stream (`/admin/boot`)**: Startup BIOS diagnostic log sequences and boot timings.
- **SEO & Favicon (`/admin/seo`)**: OpenGraph titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, and favicon paths.
- **Theme & CRT Shaders (`/admin/theme`)**: CRT scanlines, flicker effect, sound synthesizer volume, and wallpaper patterns.
- **Site Settings (`/admin/settings`)**: Master site configuration.

Equipped in Bio, Projects, Experiences, Education, and Blog studios:

- Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Highlight
- Blockquotes, Inline Code, Code Blocks
- Tables (3x3 grid, dynamic add/remove rows and columns)

- Fullscreen distraction-free writing mode
